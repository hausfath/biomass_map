# Scoping: German AD/biogas/biomethane plant-level data sources

Status: source-scoping only, nothing built yet. Prepared for the BiCRS Map German AD layer
(currently 6 hand-placed state points — not usable for siting AD+CCS).

Scope checked: all AD types — agricultural/manure, biowaste (Bioabfall), industrial
co-fermentation, sewage-sludge (Klärgas), landfill gas (Deponiegas), and biomethane
upgrading/grid-injection plants (Biomethanaufbereitung).

All sources below were actually opened (WebFetch/WebSearch/curl+pdftotext), not guessed.
Anything not independently opened is explicitly marked UNVERIFIED. Files downloaded for
verification live in the session scratchpad
(`.../scratchpad/ad_de/`) and were **not** copied into the repo (largest is 11 MB; nothing
close to the multi-GB full MaStR export was downloaded — only its file-size header and the
official documentation PDF).

## 2. Recommendation

**Primary source: MaStR Gesamtdatenexport**, specifically `EinheitenBiomasse.xml` (+ `AnlagenEegBiomasse.xml` for EEG-specific fields, + `EinheitenGasErzeuger.xml` for biomethane grid-injection units, + `Lokationen.xml` to resolve site-level location where a Lokation is shared by multiple units, e.g. satellite CHPs at one physical digester).

Why: it is the only source that is (a) plant/unit-level, (b) has real coordinates (verified), (c) covers all AD sub-types via the `Biomasseart`/`Hauptbrennstoff` catalog fields (gaseous biomass = biogas; separate categories should exist for Klär-/Deponiegas, likely coded under `EinheitenVerbrennung` or a distinct `Biomasseart` catalog value — **this exact mapping needs to be confirmed against `Katalogwerte.xml`/`Katalogkategorie.xml` before filtering**, which is cheap since those catalog files are small), and (d) is licensed for reuse (DL-DE-BY-2.0, attribution only).

**Known problem to solve before trusting a naive parse:** Fachverband Biogas's own 2025 report says raw MaStR yields 11,419 "Biogas-EEG-Anlagen" and 19,249 generation units (SEE) but the true digester count is an estimated 9,315 (on-site power) + 290 (biomethane) = 9,605, because (i) satellite CHPs are registered as their own EEG unit even though they share a digester, and (ii) the register has "zahlreiche fehlerhafte Datensätze" (numerous faulty records: wrong fuel/technology codes, missing deregistrations). Practical implication: **dedup by `Lokation`/site, not by EEG-Anlage or SEE**, and cross-check `Betriebsstatus` to drop long-dead/undecommissioned records. Budget real time for this cleaning step — it is not optional.

**Gap-fillers / cross-checks:**
- **Fachverband Biogas Branchenzahlen 2025** — national totals for QA (plant counts, installed MW, TWh) — use to sanity-check post-cleaning plant counts and capacity sums, but remember their 9,315/290 figures are themselves a corrected estimate, not a raw MaStR count, so don't expect an exact match; a match within a few percent is the right bar.
- **DBFZ Report Nr. 50** — best available substrate/manure breakdown; also useful to sanity-check the split between agricultural/waste/co-fermentation/manure-only plant categories.
- **Bavaria (LfL / Energie-Atlas)** — state-level cross-check for the largest single-state fleet; Energie-Atlas Bayern's plant-level coordinate coverage claim needs direct verification (open question, flagged above).
- **dena Biomethaneinspeisung factsheets** — cross-check biomethane injection capacity/volume trend, but do not treat as a coordinate source until biogaspartner.de's actual "Einspeiseatlas" is opened directly.

**How to obtain the data practically, given limited disk / stream-process-only:**
1. Do **not** pull the 3.1 GB full ZIP if it can be avoided. First try the Datasette-style mirror (`ds.marktstammdatenregister.dev`) with a filtered SQL/CSV export of just `EinheitenBiomasse` + `EinheitenGasErzeuger` + `Lokationen` + the small `Katalog*` tables — if it works this is by far the smallest footprint and needs no local unzip of solar/wind/nuclear data we don't want.
2. If that mirror doesn't support filtered export, use **open-mastr** with its selective/parallel download option ("Download only the tables from MaStR that you need") rather than a full bulk pull — the package explicitly supports fetching a subset of tables.
3. If both of those fail, download the official 3.1 GB ZIP once, and since a ZIP's central directory allows random access to individual members, extract only the ~5 relevant XML files (`EinheitenBiomasse.xml`, `AnlagenEegBiomasse.xml`, `EinheitenGasErzeuger.xml`, `Lokationen.xml`, the `Katalog*.xml` files) via Python `zipfile` without unpacking the full archive (solar/wind alone likely account for most of the 3 GB), then **delete the ZIP** immediately after extracting the needed members, per standing policy of not keeping raw data.
4. Expected disk footprint of the derived, cleaned biogas-only dataset: almost certainly under a few hundred MB (9,600 sites × handful of fields is trivially small; the multi-GB size is almost entirely solar PV and wind records we don't need).
5. Do **not** rely on the Zenodo snapshot as the production source — it is ~19 months stale as of today.
6. MaStR SOAP API needs registration (Marktakteur number) and is aimed at incremental/targeted lookups, not bulk export — not the right tool for an initial full build; keep in mind for future incremental updates only.

## 3. Validation totals (for sanity-checking any derived dataset)

All from **Fachverband Biogas, Branchenzahlenreport 2025** ("Stand: 10.03.2026"; plant/capacity counts as of September 2025; electricity figures are EEG-settlement year 2024), quoted verbatim/paraphrased from the primary PDF:

- Raw MaStR: **11,419 Biogas-EEG-Anlagen**, **19,249 Stromerzeugungseinheiten (SEE)** (Sept. 2025)
- Corrected estimate: **9,315** on-site-power-generation biogas plants + **290** biomethane upgrading plants = **9,605** total biogas plants (2025)
- Installed electrical capacity: **6,816 MWel** (installed) / **3,316 MWel** (Bemessungsleistung/rated) — overbuild factor ≈2.1×
- Gross electricity: **29 TWhel/yr** (28.2 TWh net/EEG-settled) → "8.8 million average households"
- Thermal: 7,798 MWtherm installed, 32.3 TWhtherm gross, 18.7 TWhtherm used externally
- Biomethane: 290 plants, **~309,239 Nm³/h** raw-gas processing capacity, **12.8 TWh** biomethane produced in 2025 (up to 14.5 TWh at full utilization); +3.5 TWh imports = 16.3 TWh available (1.9% of German gas demand; 6.3% of residential heating gas demand); usage split: transport 5.60 TWh, heat 1.07 TWh, power 8.59 TWh, other 1.04 TWh; 16 BioLNG plants, ~250,000 t capacity
- Total gross biogas+biomethane energy: **80.6 TWh** (67.8 on-site + 12.8 biomethane); stated as ~35% of Europe's biogas production (232 TWh Europe total — this comparison figure is internal to the FvB report, not independently re-verified against a European source)
- CO2 avoided: 22.3 Mt/yr
- Energy crop area 2025: 1,143,200 ha (1,051,744 ha arable + 91,456 ha grassland)
- Digestate: ~96 Mt/yr; N 735,000 t/a, P2O5 478,000 t/a, K2O 725,000 t/a
- Revenue: €8bn domestic + €1.6bn export = €9.6bn; ~67,300 jobs
- Data sources cited by FvB itself: Biogasdatenbank Fraunhofer IEE (2026), dena Branchenreport Biomethan 2025, state-level plant counts, own assumptions/corrections

From **DBFZ Report Nr. 50** (Dec 2023, data year 2021/2022):
- End 2022: **~8,500 sites** with on-site power generation (incl. satellite CHPs), installed capacity ~6.5 GW
  - ~8,250 agricultural biogas plants
  - 124 waste-digestion plants (≥90% organic-waste input)
  - ~120 co-fermentation plants
  - 1,198 EEG manure-only "Güllekleinanlagen" (≤75/70 kW), 93 MWel installed (2022; 1,040 sites / 79 MWel in 2021)
- Separately, sewage-/landfill-gas plants: 0.5 GW installed, 1.7 TWh electricity (2022) — distinct category from "Biogas"

Cross-check: FvB's 9,315 (2025) vs. DBFZ's ~8,500 (end 2022) on-site plants is a plausible growth path over ~3 years but the two use different correction methodologies — treat as order-of-magnitude agreement (~10% apart), not an exact reconciliation.

## 4. Manure-to-AD share findings

**What we could verify (primary, quantitative, from DBFZ Report Nr. 50):**
For the on-site-power-generation biogas fleet, reference year 2021:
- Total substrate input: **~65 million t FM (fresh matter) Wirtschaftsdünger (manure)** + ~61 Mt FM NawaRo (energy crops) + 2–3 Mt FM municipal biowaste + 3–4 Mt FM other organic residues
- Manure breakdown (end 2021): 43.8 Mt FM cattle slurry (Rindergülle), 7.5 Mt FM cattle solid manure (Rinderfestmist), 6.7 Mt FM pig slurry (Schweinegülle), 0.4 Mt FM pig solid manure, 6.5 Mt FM other/poultry/mixed excrement
- Substrate-mix distribution among agricultural biogas plants (mass-based): the majority use ≥30% manure input; ~44% of agricultural plants run a 31–50% manure / 49–69% NawaRo mix; ~1,700 plant sites run ≥80% manure (incl. the 1,198–1,198 EEG "Güllekleinanlagen" plus ~600 further sites); ~700 plants are "pure manure" (100% Gülle/Mist input)
- Aggregate national substrate split across the surveyed on-site-power fleet (n=413 survey respondents, 2022 data): **mass-based ≈45% animal excrement (manure), 49% NawaRo, 3% municipal biowaste, 3% industrial/commercial/agricultural residues**; energy-based the manure share drops to **≈18%** (lower energy density per tonne than energy crops), NawaRo rising to ≈73%.
- Methodological caveat: this is a **10%-response-rate operator survey**, extrapolated to the national fleet — not a census. Treat percentages as DBFZ's best estimate, not an exact accounting.

**What we could NOT verify to primary-source level:** the widely repeated "~30% of German manure/slurry is digested in biogas plants" figure (seen on UBA's own topic page, biogas.fnr.de, and multiple press outlets) has **no primary citation attached on any of the pages we opened** — UBA's page states it as fact without sourcing it to a report or table. This figure should trace methodologically to the **Thünen Report series by Rösemann et al.** ("Calculations of gaseous and particulate emissions from German agriculture 1990–20XX," the Report-on-Methods-and-Data that underlies both the National Inventory Report (NIR) and the Informative Inventory Report (IIR)), which we confirmed (via the IIR's manure-management page, UBA, 2023 edition) explicitly models anaerobic digestion of manure as a distinct manure-management system, and has recently (per the IIR text) shifted its input variable from "% of manure N digested" to "absolute amount of N sent to digestion" — implying the underlying report does contain exact digestion tonnages/shares, but **we did not open the specific Thünen Report PDF (e.g. Report 112, or the most recent RMD/IIR submission) to pull the actual current number and animal-category breakdown**, given time budget. This is the single most important open item for the manure-to-AD "nice to have."

**Label discipline:** the "~30%" figure should be carried in any downstream product as **UNVERIFIED / needs primary-source confirmation**, not stated as fact, until the Thünen Report table is opened directly. The DBFZ 65 Mt FM manure-digested figure is verified but is a mass quantity, not a percentage — computing a percentage would additionally require a verified total national manure production figure (not sourced in this pass) as denominator; do not compute or publish a percentage from these two numbers without that denominator also being sourced.

## 5. Open questions / risks

1. **MaStR record counts, coordinate completeness %, and the exact `Biomasseart`/catalog mapping for Klärgas/Deponiegas/Biomethan** are still inferred from one sample record + the doc's field list, not measured across the dataset. Before building, either (a) get the Datasette-style mirror (`ds.marktstammdatenregister.dev`) working for filtered SQL counts, or (b) do a first small extraction pass and count directly. This also settles whether coordinates are ever withheld (e.g., for very small or newly-registered units) — our one spot-check (80 kW) had coordinates, but n=1 is not proof of universal coverage.
2. **The privacy rule for natural-person operators.** The 142-page export doc only vaguely references "Datenschutzgründe"; the actual rule is in the Marktstammdatenregisterverordnung (MaStRV), which we did not pull. Biogas operators are overwhelmingly GbRs/companies rather than private individuals, so we expect limited impact, but this is an assumption, not a verified fact.
3. **Dedup strategy detail**: satellite-CHP inflation and stale/undeleted records mean a naive "one row = one plant" parse of `EinheitenBiomasse`/`AnlagenEegBiomasse` will overcount real digesters by roughly 20–25% (per FvB's own 11,419→9,315 and 19,249 SEE reconciliation). Plan to group by `Lokation`/site and filter `Betriebsstatus`/decommission dates.
4. **Klär-/Deponiegas plants** may sit partly outside `EinheitenBiomasse` (DBFZ treats them as a separate 0.5 GW / 1.7 TWh category, distinct from "Biogas"); confirm which MaStR unit-type table actually carries sewage-sludge and landfill-gas CHPs before assuming `EinheitenBiomasse` alone is complete for full AD-type coverage.
5. **dena's actual "Einspeiseatlas"** (a page on biogaspartner.de, not the CNG/LNG station map we were redirected to) was not opened directly this session — its coordinate coverage and licence terms for biomethane injection point locations remain unconfirmed. Worth one direct follow-up since it could be a useful independent geocoding cross-check specifically for the 290 biomethane plants.
6. **Bavaria's Energie-Atlas** plant-level coordinate coverage (claimed via search synthesis to cover >500,000 renewable installations including biogas) was not independently opened — flagged UNVERIFIED, worth checking directly given Bavaria's outsized share of the German fleet (2,702 gas-generation units / 1,509 MW as of 31.12.2025 per the verified LfL page).
7. **Manure-to-AD share**: as detailed in §4, the commonly cited ~30% figure is unsourced everywhere we found it; the Thünen Report (Rösemann et al.) is the right primary source to open next but was not opened this session.
8. **Freshness of any pre-built extract**: the only pre-processed community dataset found (Zenodo, open-mastr) is ~19 months stale; do not use it as-is for a "current" plant inventory — always re-derive from a fresh MaStR pull.
