### 1.2 Recommended primary source + gap-fillers + dedup strategy (Denmark)

- **Primary spine:** Energistyrelsen's **"Liste over biogasanlæg 2025"** (source #1) — it is the only genuinely close-to-complete national census (~150 plants, all categories: agricultural, sewage, landfill, industrial). Its fatal weakness is that it is name-only, no coordinates/address/capacity.
- **Gap-filler #1 (capacity + rough location):** Join by fuzzy name/company match against **EPT stamdata** (source #3) to pull postcode/town/municipality and CHP capacity for the subset of plants that also combust biogas on-site for power/heat. Expect a partial match (not all ~150 plants will have a corresponding EPT unit, since pure upgraders without local CHP won't appear).
- **Gap-filler #2 (grid-injection status & production):** Use Evida's connected-plant count (57) and Energinet system data to flag which plants are biomethane **upgraders feeding the grid** vs. CHP-only. This distinction matters directly for AD+CCS: CO₂ capture from upgrading (a concentrated CO₂ stream) is a fundamentally different (cheaper) capture case than CO₂ capture from CHP flue gas.
- **Gap-filler #3 (validation/cross-check, not for coordinates):** Biogas Danmark's 78 members can serve as a cross-check that a plant is real/active, but should not be treated as more authoritative than the ENS list because it is membership-based, not a census.
- **Manual geocoding required:** because source #1 has no address, plan on manually geocoding by plant name (many are named after the town/parish they sit in, e.g. "Lemvig Biogas," "Sindal Biogas") using Danish CVR (business registry) or municipal planning records (`plst.dk/plantemaer/tekniske-anlaeg/biogasanlaeg` was seen in search results as a possible planning-authority layer, **not independently opened** — worth checking) as a next step. Do not guess coordinates from name alone without a secondary confirming source (CVR address, or Energistyrelsen's static map image cross-referenced visually).
- **Dedup key suggestion:** `plant name (normalized) + municipality`. Where the EPT file gives multiple engine/unit rows for the same `anlaeg_navn` (e.g., Fangel Bioenergi has 4 rows for different Jenbacher engines), collapse to one plant record and sum capacity.
- **CCS-relevant subset:** BioCirc's 8 biogas plants (upgraders) are the clearest concrete AD+CCS candidates in Denmark today, given the confirmed NECCS award and trade-press reporting of a multi-site CCS rollout. This warrants a dedicated small manual entry / verification pass (call BioCirc or check CVR) rather than folding it uncritically into the auto-derived layer, since the specific site list is currently only secondary-sourced.

### 1.4 Manure-to-AD findings (Denmark) — **treat with caution, mostly unverified**

Multiple values appeared across searches, all consistent in direction (rising over time) but **none independently confirmed against a primary quoted sentence** in this session:
- ~20% of Danish livestock manure digested, 2020 — **UNVERIFIED / secondary** (WebSearch synthesis, no primary quote captured).
- ~30%, 2022 — **UNVERIFIED / secondary**.
- ~35%, as of June 2024, reported by LandbrugsAvisen — **UNVERIFIED / secondary press**, best-attested of the group but still not a primary-source quote.
- Government/Klimafremskrivning target: 60% by 2030; Biogas Danmark's own aspirational figure of up to 70% by 2030 if production reaches 50 PJ; sector climate-partnership target of 70% by 2040 — **all UNVERIFIED / secondary**, framed as targets/aspirations, not current facts.
- What IS primary-verified: manure = ~75-77% of the **feedstock mix** at existing agricultural plants (Energistyrelsen and the EA Energianalyse/Greenpeace-commissioned 2022 scenario report, which I opened directly and which states husdyrscenariet biomass input is 77% gylle+dybstrøelse, digesting ~34-43 million tonnes/yr of manure and deep litter in its 2030 scenarios). This is a different, complementary metric to "% of national manure digested" and should not be substituted for it.

**Recommendation:** before publishing any manure-to-AD % for Denmark, read the DCE NIR's agricultural/manure-management chapter directly (source #6) — that is the authoritative number DCE reports to UNFCCC, and it was not opened in this pass due to document size. This is the single most important open item for Denmark.

### 2.2 Recommended primary source + gap-fillers + dedup strategy (Netherlands)

- **Primary spine: RVO SDE(+)(+) "Projecten in beheer"** (source #1). This is the strongest single source found for the Netherlands: it is the only one with **address-level** data for a majority of records, spans all AD sub-types (mono-mest, co-digestion/"allesvergisting," RWZI sludge digestion, thermal pressure hydrolysis), and covers 2008–2026.
  - **Filter:** `Thema` contains `vergist`, `RWZI`, or `slibgisting` (651 rows found; refine further by excluding non-AD RWZI themes if any slip through, e.g. pure "RWZI verbeterde slibgisting" is AD-relevant and should stay in).
  - **Dedup:** group by `(Aanvrager Naam, Postcode, Plaats)` where not anonymized; for anonymized rows, group by `(Postcode[4-digit], Plaats, Thema-family)` and treat multiple SDE rounds at the same location as one physical plant, keeping the earliest `Ingebruikname jaar` as commissioning date and flagging expansions.
  - **Precision caveat to carry into the map:** ~48% of AD-subsidy rows are anonymized (mostly small on-farm monomestvergisting run by sole proprietors/VOFs) — these can only be placed at **postcode-4 / town centroid** precision, not exact coordinates. This should be visually distinguished on the map (e.g., a different marker/uncertainty radius) rather than silently plotted as if exact.
  - **Coverage gap:** SDE++ is voluntary — any AD plant built without ever claiming this subsidy (fully self-funded, or funded through provincial schemes only) will be invisible in this source. Cross-check against CBS's total location counts (95 mestvergisting locations in 2020, ~70 RWZI-with-biogas, ~40 "overig") to gauge coverage: 651 SDE rows (award instances, likely a few hundred unique sites) vs. CBS's ~95+70+40 ≈ 205 "location" count as of ~2020-2023 suggests the SDE register, once deduplicated, is plausibly a near-complete or even over-complete (multiple awards per site) list for the subsidized fraction — but this comparison is approximate and should be sanity-checked once real dedup is done, not assumed.
- **Gap-filler for exact coordinates on anonymized farm sites:** none identified with confidence this session. The most promising unresolved lead is **Emissieregistratie's company-report layer** (source #6) — worth a dedicated follow-up to determine if it individually geocodes mestvergisters.
- **Validation-only sources:** CBS longread (#2) and StatLine (#3) — national totals only, use for sanity-checking counts/production, not for locations.
- **CO₂-upgrading subset:** the SDE register's `Groen gas` category (459 rows total, of which a large share are `Allesvergisting`/`Monomestvergisting … ombouw naar gas` themes — i.e., biogas-to-biomethane upgrading conversions) is the right filter to identify Dutch AD sites already upgrading biogas, which is where a concentrated (cheaper-to-capture) CO₂ stream exists for AD+CCS — analogous to the Danish BioCirc case.

## 3. Cross-cutting open questions and risks

1. **Neither country's "share of manure digested" figure is primary-verified in this pass.** Denmark's figures (20% → 30% → 35%, trending toward 60-70% targets) and the Netherlands' single figure (~5% in 2022) are all secondary/press. Given how central this is to the deliverable, this is the top follow-up priority — specifically DCE's NIR manure-management annex (Denmark) and RIVM's NEMA report or a CBS mest-and-mineralen digestion-route table (Netherlands).
2. **Denmark has no free coordinate source.** The only genuinely-close-to-complete list (Energistyrelsen's ~150-plant PDF) is name-only. Any DK layer we build will require a manual/semi-manual geocoding pass (e.g., via CVR business-registry addresses), which is a real cost to budget for before committing to a launch date.
3. **The Netherlands' best source (RVO SDE register) has a structural anonymization gap** for roughly half of AD-relevant rows (small farm-scale monomestvergisting), which happens to be exactly the segment most relevant to "manure to AD" — the smallest, most numerous, most manure-dominated plants are the ones with masked addresses. Coordinate precision for these will be postcode/town-level at best unless Emissieregistratie's point-source layer (unresolved lead) turns out to cover them.
4. **Definitional/unit mismatches to watch for:** Dutch "raw biogas" PJ (CBS, ~8.1 PJ 2023) is not the same quantity as "groen gas"/biomethane m³ (Netbeheer Nederland, 336 million m³ 2025) — these differ in both scope (raw vs. upgraded) and whether they include CHP-combusted gas that's never upgraded. Similarly for Denmark, Evida's "delivered" 649 million Nm³ (2024) vs. a separately-seen "produced" 672 million Nm³ (2024, unverified) figure need reconciling before any single number is used in validation.
5. **BioCirc's CCS site list (Denmark) is only secondary/trade-press sourced** beyond the DEA's confirmed award to "BioCirc CO2 ApS." Before placing CCS-feasible markers at Favrskov/Grønhøj/Vinkel, get a primary confirmation (BioCirc's own investor materials, or a direct site visit/CVR check).
6. **RVO's SDE register itself flags a legal constraint** (2023 Rotterdam court ruling) limiting what RVO may publish for CCS-category projects specifically — worth being aware of if the BiCRS project later wants Dutch CCS project-level data from the same register, since precedent exists for redaction.
7. Sources explicitly ruled out as not useful for a national plant census: **PDOK/Omgevingsloket** (permit-checker, not an inventory) and, likely, **Biogas Danmark's membership list** (partial, self-selected, not a census — useful only for validation/cross-checking).
8. Sources flagged as promising but **not yet drilled into** (recommended next steps, roughly in priority order): (a) DCE NIR manure-management chapter [DK manure share], (b) RIVM NEMA / a CBS mest-digestion-route StatLine table [NL manure share], (c) Emissieregistratie's individual company-report/point-source layer [NL coordinates for the anonymized SDE rows], (d) Energinet's system-data portal for a named biomethane-injection-point list [DK grid-injector coordinates], (e) Danish municipal/CVR business-registry lookup for the ~150-plant name list [DK geocoding].
